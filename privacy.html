Our privacy promise

  • Your lectures stay on your device. Speech is turned into text on your phone, iPad or laptop, not on our servers.
  • No emotion tracking. Ever. Katchy only reacts to things you do, like tapping the button.
  • Nothing goes to your university. We never share your data with professors or institutions.
  • The waitlist only stores what you type: your email and, if you add it, your university. We use it only to email you about Katchy's launch, and delete it whenever you ask.

Want your email removed? Reply “stop” to any of our emails, or reach us through our contact page.

This is a plain-language summary for early access. A full privacy notice will be published before the app launches.
