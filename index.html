How it works

Three steps. Nobody else in the room even notices.

1

Open Katchy in class

It transcribes the lecture on your phone, iPad or laptop, in Italian or English, where your university allows it.

2

Tap “I zoned out”

One big button. Picked up your phone? Katchy offers to catch you up when you're back.

3

Recap + one question

Read what you missed, answer one question, then mark it: Got it, Shaky or Still lost.

FAQ

When does Katchy launch?

We're testing with students in Milan this semester. Waitlist members get the first invites, before the public launch.

How much will it cost?

Early access is free. If we ever add paid features, waitlist members will hear first and keep a free way to use the core button.

Which devices will it work on?

iPhone and iPad first, with a Windows version for laptops in testing.

Is it allowed to record my lectures?

Personal-study recording is generally tolerated in Italy, but each university and professor can set their own rules. Ask your professor; Katchy keeps everything on your device either way.

Will you spam me?

No. You'll get a handful of emails about the launch at most, and every one has an unsubscribe option.
